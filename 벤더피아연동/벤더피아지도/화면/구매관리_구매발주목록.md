## 표(그리드)

### `oGrid` — Infragistics WebDataGrid · 열 20개(보임 18) · 불러온 줄 있음: 예
- 기능: selection, activation, columnResizing, editingCore, clipboard, events
- 줄 안 요소: `INPUT¦checkbox¦ctl#$Contents$oGrid$it#_#$chkItem¦`
